RECOMMENDED MODEL BY TRAINING COMPONENT:
---------------------------------------
1. Stage 1 (Embeddings) - Text Summarization:
   COST-EFFICIENT: GPT-3.5 Turbo ($18-20)
   BALANCED: GPT-4o mini ($50-60)
   HIGHEST QUALITY: GPT-4o ($200-230)

2. Stage 3 (Cluster Labeling & Domain Organization):
   * ALWAYS USE GPT-4o ($2-6 total cost)
   * The quality improvement is significant and cost difference minimal
   * Proper labeling is critical for entire system's usability

OVERALL RECOMMENDATION:
---------------------
HYBRID APPROACH - Best cost/performance balance:
* Stage 1: GPT-3.5 Turbo for summarization
* Stage 3: GPT-4o for labeling/organization
* Total cost: ~$20-25 per training run

For exceptional quality on ALL components (~$60 premium):
* Use GPT-4o mini for Stage 1
* Use GPT-4o for Stage 3
