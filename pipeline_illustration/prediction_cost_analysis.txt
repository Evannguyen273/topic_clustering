5. KEY INSIGHTS
============
1. Text summarization represents 92-97% of prediction costs
2. Claude 3 Haiku offers the most economical approach at just $85.41/year for all centers
3. Direct LLM classification is 77× more expensive than the optimized model-based approach
4. Cost savings increase dramatically with incident volume
5. The recommended approach (Claude 3 Haiku summarization + model inference) offers:
   - 77× lower cost than direct LLM classification
   - Consistent, deterministic predictions
   - Millisecond-level response times
   - Scale-efficient architecture that handles growth well
