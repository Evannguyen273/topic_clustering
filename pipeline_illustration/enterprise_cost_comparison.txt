KEY OBSERVATIONS:
----------------
1. GPT-3.5 Turbo remains the most cost-effective option by far:
   - ~4× cheaper than Direct LLM approach
   - ~3× cheaper than GPT-4o mini
   - ~10× cheaper than GPT-4o

2. Direct LLM approach becomes more cost-competitive at enterprise scale:
   - Cheaper than full GPT-4o model-based approach
   - No training overhead or maintenance
   - Still 2× more expensive than GPT-4o mini based approach

3. Hybrid Recommendation for Enterprise Deployment:
   - Use GPT-3.5 Turbo for text summarization (stage 1)
   - Use GPT-4o for cluster labeling (stage 3)
   - Process high-confidence predictions with model (95%)
   - Process low-confidence predictions with Direct LLM (5%)
   - Estimated annual cost: ~$2,200 with very high quality
