4. IMPACT ASSESSMENT
==================

ADVANTAGES OF BIANNUAL TRAINING:
-------------------------------
1. 50% reduction in training costs (approximately $550-$5,500 annual savings)
2. Reduced operational overhead (fewer training jobs to manage and monitor)
3. More time between cycles to gather sufficient new data for meaningful model updates
4. Less risk of model thrashing (frequent model changes causing inconsistent predictions)

DISADVANTAGES OF BIANNUAL TRAINING:
---------------------------------
1. Slower adaptation to emerging incident categories and terminology
2. Performance degradation between training cycles (model drift)
3. Longer period before fixing any quality issues discovered in production
4. Risk of accumulating noise in the prediction registry between retraining

RECOMMENDED IMPLEMENTATION:
-------------------------
- Schedule biannual training at fixed dates (e.g., January and July)
- Implement continuous monitoring to detect model drift
- Add trigger for ad-hoc training cycle if:
  * Noise classification exceeds 30% of predictions
  * Significant new products/services are launched
  * Subject matter experts report decline in classification quality
- Maintain flexibility to run additional training cycles when needed
- Consider incremental training approach where new data supplements existing data
