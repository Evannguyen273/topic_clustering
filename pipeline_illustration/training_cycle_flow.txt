KEY SAFETY PRINCIPLES:
---------------------
1. Each stage writes artifacts to its own cycle-specific folder
2. The "latest_training_cycle.json" pointer is ONLY updated upon full successful completion
3. If any stage fails, new training starts with a new cycle ID, leaving previous artifacts intact
4. The model manifest provides a comprehensive inventory of all training artifacts
